Give your storefront fast, typo-tolerant search by indexing your Swell products in Algolia.

## Features

- **Always up to date.** New and edited products are sent to Algolia automatically. Products you deactivate or delete are removed from search.
- **Sync on demand.** Push your whole catalog to Algolia from **Products → Algolia sync**, or only the products created on or after a date you choose. Pick a page size, follow the progress, and stop or resume at any time.
- **Search-ready records.** Each product is indexed with its name, description, price, SKU, images, stock status and attributes, plus its full category paths for filters and hierarchical menus. Translations are included when your store has more than one language.

## Setup

1. Install the app.
2. Copy your **Application ID** and **Admin API Key** from Algolia → Settings → API Keys.
3. Paste them into the app settings and choose the index name (default: `products`).
4. Open **Products → Algolia sync** and click **Start sync** to index your current catalog.

Only active products are indexed. Keep the sync page open until the sync is complete.
